# CHEENI (चीनी) — PERMANENT CHARACTER DESIGN GUIDE (LOCKED)

> **ABSOLUTE PRIORITY RULE:** The supplied original pixel-art reference image takes precedence over all assumptions. Never redesign Cheeni's face, make him younger, alter his build, change his outfit, or add stereotypical elderly props (no cane, walking stick, glasses, cap, sandals, or traditional attire).

## 1. Facial Identity

* **Expression:** Focused, seasoned, resolute, and quietly confident. Eyes locked forward (RIGHT) toward the opponent with calm alertness rather than panic or aggression.

* **Head Shape:** Broad, sturdy oval-to-square skull with a prominent forehead, defined cheekbones, sturdy nose, and firm jaw.

* **Eyes & Brows:** Deep-set dark eyes with small white sclera highlights, heavy/defined brow ridge, and subtle age-appropriate under-eye creases.

* **Identity Lock:** Must always read unmistakably as the exact same elderly South Asian man across every frame and batch.

## 2. Age-Related Facial Characteristics

* **Forehead & Temple:** Naturally receding hairline with exposed smooth-weathered crown and slight brow lines.

* **Mid-Face:** Subtle nasolabial folds framing the moustache and nose; matured skin shading around the cheeks and under-eye areas.

* **Jawline:** Slight softening along the lower cheek and chin, grounded by light grey stubble.

* **Visual Balance:** Clearly elderly (late 60s to 70s visual read), yet alert, sturdy, and physically composed — never frail, sickly, or caricatured.

## 3. Hair and Facial Hair

* **Scalp Hair:** Balding across the top and front crown; short, closely cropped silver-white and light-grey hair wrapping around the sides (temples, above the ears) and the back of the head.

* **Moustache:** Distinct, medium-thick trimmed white/silver-grey moustache curving neatly over the upper lip.

* **Beard / Stubble:** Light, short grey-white stubble tracing the chin and lower jawline (no full beard).

* **Hair Color Ramp:** Highlight `#ECECEC` / `#D8D8D8`, Midtone `#B0B0B0`, Shadow `#787878`.

## 4. Skin Tone and Colours

* **Base Tone:** Medium-deep warm South Asian brown/tan skin with rich terracotta undertones.

* **Reference Hex Palette (Sampled from Original Asset):**

  * **Skin Highlight:** `#D08761` (forehead, cheekbones, deltoids, forearms, knees, tops of feet)

  * **Skin Midtone:** `#B77E5B` / `#B26D4B` (primary limbs, face, neck)

  * **Skin Shadow:** `#8A4835` / `#6E3526` (underside of arms, inner calves, neck shadow, fist crevices)

* **Consistency Rule:** Maintain warm brown skin ramp across all lighting angles; never desaturate to pale/grey or shift to orange.

## 5. Clothing and Accessories

* **Top — Plain White Crew-Neck T-Shirt:**

  * Short-sleeved, relaxed/casual fit draping naturally over his slightly rounded midsection.

  * Small dark-grey/black horizontal rectangular mark/emblem on the upper-center chest (partially behind the rear guard forearm in default stance).

  * **Color Ramp:** Bright White `#FFFFFF` / `#F4F4FA`, Cool Periwinkle-Grey Mid-Shadow `#CDC7DA` / `#C4C1D6`, Deep Fold Shadow `#9895B0`.

* **Bottom — Cream/Grey Checkered Casual Shorts:**

  * Mid-thigh length, loose everyday home/boxer-style shorts featuring a clean light cream/off-white base with a subtle olive-grey / warm-grey grid/checkered pattern.

  * **Color Ramp:** Cream Base `#ECE9DD`, Checkered Grid Midtone `#B3AD9D`, Shadow `#8E8878` / `#A9A392`.

* **Footwear:** **BAREFOOT.** No shoes, no socks, no slippers, no sandals. Individual toes cleanly pixel-rendered on both feet.

* **Accessories:** **NONE.** Strictly zero glasses, watch, rings, cane, stick, hat, or belt.

## 6. Character Height and Body Proportions

* **Reference Proportions:** Foreground bounding box in original reference is `995 × 1895 px` (width-to-height aspect ratio `0.525`).

* **Head-to-Body Ratio:** Approximately **5.5 to 6.0 heads tall** in fighting stance (compact, grounded, sturdy proportions — distinct from taller, leaner younger fighters like Sagar or Rahul).

* **Build:** Stocky, solid, everyday elderly frame with a modest natural paunch/belly, sturdy limbs, and strong planted legs. Never slim him down into an athletic youth silhouette or exaggerate him into a caricature.

## 7. Shoulder and Torso Structure

* **Shoulders:** Broad enough to look capable, set with a natural slight forward-rounding typical of an older man in a guard posture.

* **Torso:** Full, relaxed midsection visible through the drape and gentle lower-belly curve of the white t-shirt.

* **Back & Spine:** Upright and controlled with a subtle natural forward fighting tilt — never hunched over, crippled, or slumped.

## 8. Arm and Leg Proportions

* **Arms:** Sturdy, medium-thick forearms and upper arms with visible elbow structure and solid clenched fists (not overly muscular, not spindly).

* **Legs:** Solid thighs and defined, sturdy calves with natural knee articulation and grounded, broad bare feet with visible toes.

* **Limb Continuity:** Preserve exact relative lengths of upper arms, forearms, thighs, and shins across standing, crouching, dashing, and jumping frames.

## 9. Posture and Body Characteristics

* **Center of Gravity:** Low, balanced, and firmly planted over his feet.

* **Movement Philosophy:** Experienced, economical, controlled body mechanics. Slight anatomical stiffness compared to young acrobats, balanced by smart weight transfer, careful foot placement, and surprising bursts of speed when dashing or reacting.

* **Guard Discipline:** Hands stay protective and connected to the torso; never flailing wildly.

## 10. Default Fighting Stance

* **Facing:** Facing **RIGHT** (3/4 profile toward the viewer/right).

* **Foot Placement:** Wide, stable barefoot stance — rear (right) foot planted back and angled slightly outward; lead (left) foot planted forward pointing right. Knees moderately bent.

* **Hand & Arm Guard:** Classic bare-knuckle boxing/brawler guard — lead (left) fist raised forward at chest-to-chin height; rear (right) fist tucked closer to the center chest/ribs protecting the body and chin.

* **Head Alignment:** Chin slightly tucked, eyes focused directly right.

## 11. Pixel-Art Outline and Shading Style

* **Art Style:** Crisp 16-bit / 32-bit arcade fighting-game pixel art (inspired by classic Capcom/SNK arcade sprites).

* **Outlines:** Clean, solid 1-pixel (at native art pixel scale) black/near-black (`#0B080A` / `#100C0E`) outer silhouette outline with selective darker interior contours for muscle/cloth separation.

* **Shading:** Clean 3-to-4-tone cel-shaded pixel clusters with light dithering/pixel-stepping along anatomical curves (especially skin and shirt folds). No blurry airbrushing, no muddy anti-aliasing, no soft outer glow.

* **Transparency:** Pure 32-bit RGBA PNG with genuine alpha transparency (0% alpha background, 100% crisp opaque sprite pixels, zero white/color fringe).

## 12. Character Scale

* **Frame Cell Dimensions:** `288 × 288 px` (locked for all Cheeni batches).

* **Ground Baseline:** `Y = 272 px` (bottom of planted bare feet rests flush on Y = 272).

* **Horizontal Root Pivot:** `X = 144 px` (center of mass / stance center aligned to X = 144).

* **Canonical Standing Height:** `~224 px` (head crown around `Y ≈ 48 px`, feet at `Y = 272 px`).

* **Safe Margins:** `~48 px` top headroom, `16 px` bottom floor buffer, and `~80+ px` left/right horizontal breathing room in neutral stance so dashes, jumps, and future attacks never clip frame edges.

* **Scaling Filter:** Nearest-neighbour ONLY (preserves crisp pixel edges).

## 13. Default Facing Direction

* **Canonical Direction:** **RIGHT** (`+X`) across all Batch 01–05 sprite sheets.

* Backward walking and backward dashing MUST keep Cheeni facing RIGHT (never turning his back to the opponent).

## 14. Personality and Behavioural Characteristics

* **Core Traits:** Elderly wisdom and life experience, sharp intelligence, stubborn determination, strong attachment to his property, playful confidence, and surprising martial composure.

* **Body Language:** Calm, unhurried confidence in idle; measured, deliberate steps when walking; unexpectedly sharp bursts during dashes; practical, knee-conscious crouching and landing.

* **Anti-Caricature Rule:** Never depict him trembling uncontrollably, hunched in pain, confused, or helpless. He is a capable, clever veteran fighter.

## 15. Future Sweet-Based Fighting Identity (Locked for Batch 04+)

* **Theme:** Cheeni ("Sugar" / चीनी) has a deep love of sweets that manifests in his Batch 04 special moves, traps, and super/ultimate attacks.

* **Planned Power Motifs:** Sugar crystals, molten/stretchy caramel, sticky syrup puddles/traps, candy-based projectiles, confectionery energy bursts, and sweet-themed ultimates.

* **Locked Future Accent Palette (Batch 04+ Only):**

  * Sugar White (`#FFFDF7`), Caramel Brown (`#B86B2B`), Warm Cream (`#F5E6C8`), Warm Amber (`#E69B38`), with controlled Candy Pink (`#F279A6`) and Pistachio/Mint Green (`#68C48B`) accents.

* **Batch 01 Restriction:** **ZERO** sweets, candy, syrup, or magical VFX in Batch 01 basic movement. Keep Batch 01 strictly clean, grounded fighting-game movement.

## 16. Property-Owner Story Identity

* **Story Role:** Cheeni is the rightful, central owner of the disputed property around which the entire fighting game's conflict revolves.

* **Tone:** Not a villain — he is the legitimate, stubborn, clever, and endearing elder defending what is rightfully his. His everyday home attire (white t-shirt, checkered shorts, bare feet) directly reinforces this grounded, authentic "rightful homeowner caught defending his own place" identity.
